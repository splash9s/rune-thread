Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This policy explains what data Rune Thread collects, why, who it is shared with, and how you can have it deleted. It applies to the Rune Thread game on the web at rune-thread.com and to the Rune Thread app for Android. Rune Thread is made by Marushi, an independent developer in Thailand ("we"). Questions: support@rune-thread.com.

What we collect

  • Account: an account ID. If you sign in with Google or bind an email, your email address. Guest accounts have no email. We never see your Google password.
  • Game data: your player name, avatar, characters, levels, inventory, gold, Diamonds, skills, settings, friends and friend requests, in-game mail, market listings and bids, leaderboard records, and short messages you write on friends' tents.
  • Feedback you send from the Settings menu.
  • Purchases: a record of each top-up (payment or order ID, what you bought, amount and date). We do not receive or store card or bank details; the payment provider handles them.
  • Usage and security data: which game features are used and when, and records that help us detect cheating and fraud.
  • Technical data sent automatically by your browser or device, such as IP address, device and browser type, which our hosting and security providers use to deliver the game and block abuse.
  • Android app only, for ads: Google AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and device information to show ads and measure them. We also keep a record of each ad reward you earn.

The game also saves your progress on your own device (browser storage), so it can load quickly and work offline for a while.

How we use it

  • To run the game: save and restore your progress across devices, and run friends, mail, the market and leaderboards.
  • To process purchases and give you what you bought, and to handle refunds.
  • To give rewards for watching ads (Android app).
  • To prevent cheating, fraud and abuse, and to enforce the Terms.
  • To fix bugs, improve the game, and answer support requests and feedback.

What other players can see

Your player name, avatar, level, player ID, tent and pets, market listings and leaderboard records can be seen by other players. Tent messages can be read by you and the friend you wrote to. Your email address is never shown to other players.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with these service providers, for the purposes above:

  • Supabase – database, sign-in and server functions (stores your account and game data).
  • Cloudflare – website hosting and network security, and Cloudflare Turnstile, which checks that you are a real person when you sign in.
  • Google – Google sign-in; Google Play Billing for purchases in the Android app; Google AdMob for ads in the Android app.
  • Stripe – payments on the website.

We may also disclose data if the law requires it, or to protect players and the game from fraud or abuse. Each provider handles data under its own privacy policy, for example Google, Stripe, Cloudflare and Supabase. These providers may process data outside your country.

Ads and your choices (Android app)

The Android app shows rewarded ads only when you choose to watch one. Players in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads). The website does not show ads.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to the database is limited by account, and only the developer can use the admin tools.

How long we keep data

  • Account and game data: for as long as your account exists.
  • Tent messages: 7 days. Tent visitor logs: 3 days.
  • Purchase records: as long as tax and accounting law requires, even after an account is deleted.
  • Records needed to prevent fraud or repeated cheating, such as a ban record: as long as needed for that purpose.

Delete your account and data

Email support@rune-thread.com with the subject "Delete my Rune Thread account" and include your player name and player ID (shown in the game), and send it from the email address linked to your account if you have one. We will delete your account, game data, friends, messages, feedback and ad reward records within 30 days and confirm by email. Purchase records are kept only as long as the law requires, as described above. Deleting the account cannot be undone, and items and Diamonds on it are lost.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU GDPR or Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, and to object to or limit how it is used. To use these rights, email support@rune-thread.com. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Rune Thread is not made for children under 13, and you must be at least 13 to create an account. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes. The date at the top shows the latest version, and important changes are announced in the game.
