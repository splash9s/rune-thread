Refund Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Diamonds and digital items

Diamonds, gold and other in-game items are digital goods that are delivered to your account right after payment. Once delivered, purchases are generally not refundable, except as described below or where the law where you live requires a refund.

When we refund

  • You were charged but the Diamonds or gold never arrived.
  • You were charged twice for the same purchase.
  • The law where you live gives you a right to a refund.

Please contact us within 30 days of the purchase.

Purchases on the website (Stripe)

Email support@rune-thread.com with your player name and player ID (shown in the game), the email on your receipt, and the payment reference from your Stripe receipt. We reply within 3 business days. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method.

Purchases in the Android app (Google Play)

Purchases in the app are processed by Google Play. You can ask Google Play for a refund under its own refund policy (usually within 48 hours of the purchase, directly in Google Play). After that, email us as above and include the Google Play order number from your receipt email (it starts with GPA.).

What happens after a refund

When a purchase is refunded, the Diamonds or gold it added are removed from your account the next time you sign in (never below zero). Accounts that repeatedly refund purchases after using the items may be restricted.
