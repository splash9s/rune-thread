About

Rune Thread

Rune Thread is an idle fantasy RPG inspired by Norse myth. Build your hero, choose rune blessings, clear the campaign, climb the Warded Tower with friends and trade gear on the market. Your progress is saved to your account, so you can play in the browser at rune-thread.com or in the Android app.

The game is free to play. Diamonds can be bought to speed things up, and the Android app offers optional rewarded ads.

Made by

Rune Thread is made by Marushi, an independent developer in Thailand.
